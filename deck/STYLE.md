# Deck style: learned from the PVP reference deck (SIBCC_Final_PVP.pdf), applied to the ledger palette

Reference: PVP Team, ShARE UI International BCC (54 pages: cover, 15 core, summary, thank-you, references, appendix 1–17).
The ledger (palette, fonts, text rules, page plan) overrides the reference wherever they differ.

## Chrome, core pages (light)
- Left-edge rounded pill, top-left (gradient, accent family).
- Line 1: section label, bold italic (reference: "Background", "Supply Chain Analysis", "Analysis Conclusion", "Solution #1: ...").
- Line 2: action title in sentence case, regular weight, about 13 pt, two lines max; key numbers bold.
- Top-right: white rounded tab with a soft shadow holding the text wordmark "Telkomsel · IndiHome · Orbit" (no logos).
- Footer: "Source: [1] ..." italic grey bottom-left with superscript markers; breadcrumb bottom-right
  (Background · Analysis · Solution · Implementation · Projection), current section bold and dark, others light grey;
  then a thin vertical bar and the page number.

## Page grammar
- Zones are introduced by a numbered circle badge (1, 2, 3) plus a heading sentence with a thin accent rule under it.
- Section headers inside a page are gradient banner bars or chevrons with white bold text.
- Cards: white, rounded, soft shadow; key words bold; text centred in short cards, left-aligned in long ones.
- Each pillar keeps one colour everywhere: H = accent red, O = dark red, M = ink (gradient tiles).
- Problems / "where it breaks" are flagged in red; BaU is grey, strategy is the accent (charts).
- Numbers: big bold value with a small label below, often in dashed-border boxes; metric strip as label | value pairs.
- Every page carries a visual (diagram, chart, table with coloured header row); no plain bullet pages.

## Signature pages
- Executive Summary: dark full-bleed background, white row labels on the left (Situation / Complication / Solution / Impact),
  one large white rounded panel: Situation sentence, pillar header tabs, complication cards, pillar tiles with an enlarged first
  letter, dashed impact boxes.
- Analysis Conclusion: Current Condition → Key Question → Problem → Ideal Condition bridge, then pillar-coloured
  "Solution n" bands with "Which problems to tackle? / Which solutions? / Why?".
- Solution Overview: temple device: roof banner with letter badges, three pillar columns, a left rail of Why / How / What,
  a base band with the impact.
- Implementation: governance map left; timeline (years × pillar lanes) and a risk heat map (likelihood × impact) with a
  trigger/action table right.
- Projection: BaU vs strategy bars with a difference line, BEP marked, and an NPV / IRR / B/C / BEP strip.
- Cover: dark background with a ribbon motif, acronym tinted, white bottom band with "Proposed by", team name, competition
  and member photo circles.

## Appendix
- Title "Appendix n" in bold italic, subtitle below in small regular text; no breadcrumb; page number with the bar.
- Long tables with a coloured header row; detailed models and analyses live here.
